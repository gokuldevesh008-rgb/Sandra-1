Why it matters

A quick guide to the foods in the game.

🥦 Healthy foods

  • Broccoli, carrots and salad give fibre, vitamins and minerals.
  • Apples are a sweet snack with fibre and no added sugar.
  • Fish provides protein and healthy fats.

🍔 Unhealthy foods

  • Burgers and fries are often high in salt and unhealthy fats.
  • Donuts and candy are packed with sugar and few nutrients.
  • Soda adds lots of sugar with no real nutrition.

Easy healthy habits

Small changes add up.

🌈

Eat colourful

Fill half your plate with fruits and vegetables.

💧

Drink water

🍽️
